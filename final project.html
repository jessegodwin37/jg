YOUR CHALLENGE

Create Something Real

Your final project is designed to test whether you can combine HTML, CSS and JavaScript to build a functional website.

💻 Project Brief

Create a personal portfolio website for yourself or a fictional professional.

The website should present the person's skills, experience, projects and contact information.

REQUIREMENTS

What Your Website Must Include

01

Homepage

Create a clear homepage introducing the person or business.

02

About Section

Explain who the person is and what they do.

03

Skills

Display at least three skills using attractive cards or sections.

04

Projects

Show at least two projects or examples of work.

05

Contact

Add a way for visitors to contact the person.

06

Responsive Design

Your website must work properly on mobile and desktop.

⚙️ JavaScript Requirement

Your project must contain at least one interactive feature powered by JavaScript.

  • A working mobile navigation menu
  • A button interaction
  • A form validation feature
  • A simple calculator
  • A dark/light mode switch

You only need to implement at least one of these features.
